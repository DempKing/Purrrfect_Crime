# Loot pictures

Every piece of loot in the game, and the file name its picture should have. Upload a picture as
`art/models/loot_<file name>.webp` (PNG is fine too, renamed or converted to WebP) and it replaces the drawn
one the next time the art is prepared (`node tools/prep-art.mjs`), in the cases, on the plinths, in the
fence's ledger and in the reports.

**Style:** like the existing loot pictures (`loot_bell.webp`, `loot_pharaoh.webp`...): the object alone,
three-quarter view, on a transparent background, about 512 × 512, lit from the top left.

"Now" says what it shows today: **painted** (one of your pictures), or **drawn** (a simple drawing made from
its name, until a painting arrives).
