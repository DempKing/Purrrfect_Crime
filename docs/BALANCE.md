Against that: every skill costs 21,000 Catnip, every lair room at its top tier
21,600, and every attribute rank about 9,600 before the Scratching Tower's
discount, 52,200 in all. A flawless run that takes everything earns more than all of it costs; a real first run takes less loot and is seldom flawless, so it buys most of it, and the rest comes from replays.

Second Wind needs level 22, so it's a post-game skill: about 5 more heists' worth of XP after the story.
