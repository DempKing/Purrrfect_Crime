How to play
  • Keyboard: the arrow keys move a cursor, Enter moves Leo there or does what's there, Space ends the turn, I opens the bag, R twice restarts, Q and E turn the camera, WASD pans it (C brings it back to Leo), + and − (or the mouse wheel) zoom. The ⚙ button has sound, speed and accessibility settings.
  • Controller: the D-pad or left stick moves the cursor, A goes there or does what's there, X ends the turn, Y opens the bag, LB and RB turn the camera, LT and RT zoom, the right stick pans (click a stick to come back to Leo). View/Select reaches the buttons (actions, gadgets, Restart…), Menu/Start opens the settings, and B cancels. In menus, the D-pad moves between buttons and A presses.
  • Your own controls: ⚙ Settings → Controls lets you change any key or controller button.
  • Move: click a tile. Blue costs 1 action point, yellow costs both (a loud dash). On a phone, tap once to preview and again to move.
  • Path colors: white is safe, yellow means a guard will notice something and come to look, red means caught or alarm.
  • A rival's alarm isn't yours: lie low and stay out of sight until it blows over (or shut it off at the camera panel), and your run stays flawless. If it reaches lockdown, the exits stay sealed until the guards catch the rival.
  • When the guards move: a red cross on a tile means Leo would be caught, or a camera would turn onto him, if he ended his turn there; an orange ring means he'd be spotted. Pointing at it says which.
  • Shadows hide you. Guards see 6 tiles into lamplight, 4 into moonlight and barely past their nose in the dark. Being right next to one, or standing in lamplight in front of one, gets you caught.
  • Red is what the guards see, purple is what the cameras see. Orange dots are where the guards walk next.
  • Guard types: sleepy guards (Zzz) see nothing until a noise wakes them; captains (gold braid) see further and call others over; sniffers (big nose) smell you through walls from two tiles away.
  • Pressure plates (raised metal squares) set off the alarm. Motion sensors (the red grid) allow one tile per move inside them; the fuse box turns them off.
  • Keycard doors (steel, with a card reader) open only with the level's keycard: walk over it to pick it up. Vault doors need the combination, hidden in a desk. Glass walls stop Leo and sound, but not eyes. Guard dogs bark at Leo within 2 tiles of their kennel. Wires cross rooftop gaps, with the Tightrope skill; Pounce can leap a one-tile gap.
  • Harbor: climb into an open crate and nobody sees or smells you. The lighthouse beam turns an eighth a turn. Tidal sand is under water at high tide (the HUD counts down). On the fish market floor (green shimmer) sniffers can't smell a cat. Light switches put lamps out, but a guard soon comes to put them back on.
  • Downtown: Robo-Guards never sleep and ignore noises, decoys and empty cases, but their heat scanners see Leo 4 tiles off in any light; cutting the fuse box powers them down. Slot machines drown out any noise Leo makes among them. Conveyor belts carry Leo a tile at the end of each turn. Wet paint leaves paw prints guards will follow. A time lock holds the vault shut until a set turn.
  • Rival thieves sometimes want the same centerpiece. Their purple footprints show where they're heading. Take it first and they give up; if they take it, it's gone for tonight.
  • Creaky floorboards (brown planks) are loud. Safes take three quiet goes. Spotlights move every turn. Ladders lead up to the rafters: stand on one and climb. Nobody looks up, but dropping down makes a thump (unless Leo has Ceiling Climb).
  • Noise: marble floors, dashing, picking locks and (loudest) smashing glass. Guards who hear it come to check.
  • Steal: next to a case, pick its lock (quiet, two goes) or smash it (one go, very loud). Use the buttons at the bottom, keys 1–9, or click the case.
  • Security: the camera panel shuts off the cameras and the alarm; the fuse box shuts off the lasers. A camera sighting, a broken laser or a guard finding an empty case starts a 5-turn lockdown countdown.
  • Vents: step onto a grate and climb in. Nobody sees you in the ducts, but crawling is slow.
  • Inventory: 6 spaces. Bigger pieces take up more of them.
  • Gadgets and skills (the teal row above the actions): bottle caps from the Workshop, and skills like Pounce, Camera Loop and Smoke Pellet. Click one, then click a highlighted tile. Esc cancels.
  • Loot: click a case, plinth, safe or an exhibit on a pedestal for what Leo can do: steal it, pick the lock (quiet, sometimes more than one go) or smash the glass (quick, but loud). If he isn't beside it, he walks over first.
  • Escape: walk onto the green exit with something in your inventory.
