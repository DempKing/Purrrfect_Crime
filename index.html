How to play
  • Move: click a tile. Blue tiles cost 1 action point, yellow cost both. On a phone, tap once to preview and again to move.
  • Red tiles are what the bulldog can see. Step into them and you're caught. A red path means the move would get you caught.
  • Orange dots show where the bulldog will walk on his next turn.
  • Steal: stand next to the plinth and press Steal, or click the plinth. Costs 1 action point.
  • Escape: walk onto the green exit with the Moonstone.
  • Camera: Q and E turn the view. Walls near Leo drop down so he's never hidden.
  • End turn: Space. Your turn also ends when you're out of action points.

Purrrfect Crime

Leo Tuxedo's first job: lift the Moonstone from the Grey Gallery and slip out the exit, without Brutus the night-watch bulldog spotting him.

Everything is placeholder art. This build tests the core turn loop: moving on the grid with two action points, reading the guard's vision and patrol, turning the camera, and getting caught.

  • Click a tile to move: blue 1 action point, yellow 2.
  • Red is what Brutus sees. Orange dots are where he's going next.
  • Q / E turn the camera · Space ends your turn · F steals · R restarts.
